# Community code of conduct

AgenticRAG contributors and maintainers are expected to keep participation
welcoming, professional and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience and viewpoints.
- Give actionable technical feedback and assume good intent.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes and protect user/security information.
- Keep discussions free of harassment, threats, discrimination and sexualized or
  insulting content.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior threatens people or the project.
Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports are handled as
confidentially as practical, with conflicts of interest delegated to another
maintainer.
